How I work

From the problem to the shipped release, with every decision on record.

  1. Pain points with sources

    I research what bothers the people who will use the product, with sources, and record each pain point with a priority. That record becomes the backlog.

  2. Specification

    I define clear acceptance criteria before building.

  3. Tests

    Automated tests, including security and role-based permission tests.

  4. Delivery

    Numbered releases, with migrations rehearsed on a copy of the database, backups and post-release checks.
